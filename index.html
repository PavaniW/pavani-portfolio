Questions Brands Ask

Everything you might want to know before we work together.

Still have a question? DM me on Instagram
Do you have a rate card?

Yes. Ask for it here and I'll send it straight to your inbox.

What kind of collaborations are you open to?

Paid campaigns, gifted collaborations, product launches, events, modelling and photoshoots, and restaurant, café and hotel visits. I'm also happy to talk about longer partnerships if we're a good fit.

What content do you create, and where is it posted?

Reels, TikToks, photos and stories, covering skincare, makeup, food and lifestyle. I also model for brand shoots, lookbooks and runway shows. I post on Instagram and TikTok, and can share the same piece on both or make something just for one.

Can you model for our shoot or show?

Yes. I'm training as a model at Brian Kerkoven Academy, so I'm comfortable on set and on the runway. I'm available for product shoots, campaigns, lookbooks and fashion shows. Send me the date and what you have in mind.

Where are you based? Do you travel?

I'm based in Colombo, Sri Lanka, and happy to travel, locally or abroad, for shoots, events and stays. I work with brands anywhere in the world, and I'm open to any opportunity that works well for both of us. You can also ship products to me in Colombo.

How long does it take?

Usually about a week after I receive the product or visit. If you have a launch date, let me know and I'll plan around it.

Will we see the content before it goes live?

Yes. I'll share a draft for your feedback before anything is posted, with one round of changes included.

Can we use your content on our own page or in ads?

Yes. Let me know where you'd like to use it and for how long, and I'll include it in my quote. Raw footage is available too.

Do you share results after posting?

Yes. About a week after the post goes live, I'll send you screenshots of views, reach, likes, saves and shares.

I'll also feature your brand here in my portfolio, and I can share how many visitors clicked through to you from it.

What if the product isn't a good fit for you?

I only post what I'd honestly recommend, which is why my audience trusts me. If something doesn't work for me, I'll tell you privately first instead of posting about it.
