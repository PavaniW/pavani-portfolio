Questions brands ask

How much do you charge?

It depends on what you need, like how many reels, stories or photos, and whether I post them on my page. Ask for my rate card and I'll send it by email.

What kind of content do you make?

Reels and TikToks, product photos, Instagram stories, honest reviews, restaurant and café visits, and makeup and outfit looks.

Do you post on Instagram and TikTok?

Yes. I can post the same video on both, or make something just for one.

How long does it take?

Usually about a week after I receive the product. If you have a launch date, tell me and I'll plan around it.

Can we use your content on our own page or in ads?

Yes. Tell me where you'd like to use it and for how long, and I'll include that in my quote.

What if I don't like your product?

I only post what I'd honestly recommend. If something doesn't work for me, I'll tell you privately first instead of posting about it.

Do you work with brands outside Colombo?

Yes. You can send products to me anywhere in Sri Lanka, and I visit restaurants and cafés around Colombo.
